## Rally2 WRC Kit

The 2027 Rally2 WRC Kit is now available under **Prototype / Testing**. It starts with the Rally2 EVO tarmac car and its five-speed Type-A gearset. The Kit leaves out the 10 kg of front ballast fitted to the Rally2 Tarmac Balanced setup.

- The Kit has its own front bumper, front fenders, front lip and larger rear wing. I moved their nodes to match the bodywork and revised the aero on the front lip and rear wing.

- The Kit uses the Rally2 engine, turbo and ECU with a 33 mm restrictor in place of the 32 mm one. Peak power rises from **290 to 307 PS**. The gain builds from about 11 PS at 5000 rpm to 19 PS at 6000 and 22 PS at 7000. The WRC16 shares the 33 mm restrictor and stays at about 310 PS with its own ECU calibration.

- The Rally2 ECU now picks the shift lights for the fitted gearset and restrictor. With the Kit's 33 mm restrictor and Type-A gears, the four upshift flashes are **6500, 6680, 6630 and 6530 rpm**. The Kit has its own page in the [gearing and shift-light workbook](../../gearing_shiftlights.xlsx).
